Why Kids & Parents Love Our Camps

100% Hands-On Cooking

No watching from afar! Every camper chops, stirs, measures, and cooks their own individual delicious dishes.

Safety & Allergen Conscious

Strictly nut-aware facility with gluten-free & vegetarian accommodations, plus pediatric first-aid certified chef staff.

Friday Family Feast

Camp culminates in our Friday Chef Showcase where young chefs present dishes they cooked to their proud parents!

A Day in the Life of a Young Chef

Morning Chef Prep

Campers wash up, tie on aprons, sharpen kid-safe knife techniques, and learn the day’s recipe science and kitchen math.

Cooking & Creation

Campers work at their dedicated stations to prepare two savoury entrees and one baked dessert from scratch.

Tasting & Community

Everyone sits together in our dining hall to savor their creations, write tasting notes, and add recipes to their binder.
