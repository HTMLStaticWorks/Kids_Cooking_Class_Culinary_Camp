The Story Behind Kids Cooking

Founded in 2018 by professional chef Antonio Rossi and early childhood educator Claire Beaumont, Kids Cooking was created to transform the way children interact with food.

Instead of artificial demonstrations where kids only observe, every class at Kids Cooking puts spoons, rolling pins, and fresh organic produce directly into students’ hands. From cracking their first egg to mastering safe sautéing, our students discover pride and joy in every meal.

Today, we have welcomed over 12,000 young chefs across our weekend workshops, after-school academies, and summer culinary camps.
