How Kids Cooking Works

1. Choose Age Group

Select classes or summer camps customized for ages 4–6, 7–10, 11–13, or teens.

2. Book in Parent Portal

Reserve time slots easily, list any food allergies, and track all bookings in your dashboard.

3. Apron Up & Cook

Young chefs get their own kid-safe station, ingredients, and guidance from friendly professional chefs.

4. Feast & Take Home

Kids taste their dishes together, receive printed recipe cards, and take leftovers home to share!
