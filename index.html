Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers to common parent questions about class age groups, dietary accommodations, observation policies, and what to bring.

We welcome young culinary enthusiasts ages 4 to 17! Our sessions are thoughtfully organized into four age tiers: Tiny Bakers (ages 4–6), Junior Chefs (ages 7–10), Tween Culinary (ages 11–13), and Teen Gourmet Academy (ages 14–17). Each tier features age-tailored recipes, specialized safety tools, and matched chef instructor guidance.

Yes, absolutely! Our kitchen studio is strictly nut-aware (100% peanut and tree-nut free). When you enroll, you specify any allergies on your child's profile. We provide color-coded cutting boards, sanitized cookware, and dedicated allergen-free ingredient stations for gluten-free, dairy-free, and vegetarian dietary requirements.

Children should wear comfortable casual clothing, closed-toe rubber-soled shoes (sneakers are perfect), and have longer hair pulled back. We provide clean, sanitized child-sized chef aprons, chef hats, all ingredients, kid-safe tools, and takeout containers. You don't need to bring any equipment!

For our Tiny Chefs (ages 4–6), parents are welcome to relax in our glass-partitioned observation lounge with complimentary espresso and high-speed Wi-Fi. For children ages 7 and up, classes are drop-off to build independence, focus, and teamwork. Parents are warmly invited in for the final 15 minutes for our presentation and tasting showcase!

We know busy family schedules change! Single weekend workshops can be rescheduled with at least 48 hours notice for full studio credit. For multi-week academy courses and summer culinary camps, we offer up to 2 flexible makeup sessions per semester during our open studio hours.

Both! At the end of every class, students gather at our communal dining banquette to taste and celebrate the dishes they prepared. Any extra portions and bakery projects are packed into eco-friendly takeout boxes complete with step-by-step recipe cards to proudly share with family at home.

Our friendly studio team is happy to answer any questions about schedules, group bookings, or custom party packages.
